## Brand & Style

This design system defines an intelligence observatory and editorial dashboard at the intersection of developer infrastructure and tech publication craft. Drawing inspiration from modern engineering cockpits like Linear, Raycast, and Vercel alongside the typographic precision of Wired and The Verge, the visual language prioritizes high-signal information density without sacrificing sensory refinement.

### Personality & Tone
- **Atmosphere:** An authoritative, live terminal observing the technological frontier in real-time.
- **Tone:** Direct, technical, analytical, and discerning. It filters signal from noise with zero ambient clutter.
- **Target Audience:** Applied AI researchers, founders, staff-level engineers, systems architects, and intelligence analysts.

### Design Movement: Dark Technical Minimalist & Glass Precision
- **Deep Obsidian Canvas:** Pitch-dark space layered with hairline micro-grids, geometric coordinates, and subtle radial luminescence.
- **Micro-Engineered Double Borders:** Translucent containers featuring crystalline 1px refractive highlights on top edges and subdued dark drop shadows.
- **Tabular Precision:** Tight alignment of monospace metadata with fluid editorial headlines to create an unmistakable "flight deck" feel.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for sustained telemetry monitoring in dark environments. Absolute pitch obsidian serves as the non-distracting bedrock, while chromatic energy is strictly reserved for actionable signals, model inference indicators, and telemetry states.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Canvas Foundation (`neutral` / `#07080b`):** Deep space obsidian. Absorbs eye fatigue during extended deep-work sessions while letting glass containers articulate elevation.
- **Radar Emerald (`primary` / `#10b981`):** The primary pulse. Dictates active live ingestion, critical breakthrough flags ("Must Read"), operational health pings, and definitive user actions.
- **Velocity Cyan (`secondary` / `#0ea5e9`):** Engine telemetry, model provenance tags (e.g., Groq LLaMA-3, Claude Opus), and external web anchors.
- **Pulse Violet (`tertiary` / `#8b5cf6`):** Multi-agent coordination streams, deep research preprints, and synthetic data synthesis notes.

### Semantic Tones & Accents
- **Amber Warning (`accent-amber` / `#f59e0b`):** Rate-limit cooldowns, speculative findings, and circuit breaker trip alerts.
- **Critical Alert (`accent-coral` / `#f87171`):** Quota failure, crawl interruption, and broken ingestion pipelines.
- **Mint Luminescence (`mint-luminescence` / `#a7f3d0`):** Specialized high-legibility green for reading the "Why It Matters" strategic intelligence callouts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the human geometric clarity of **Outfit** for editorial titles and readable abstracts with the structural discipline of **JetBrains Mono** for provenance metrics, system keys, and sentiment scores.

### Tracking and Rhythm
- **Negative Tracking on Headlines:** Tight character cohesion (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) produces an authoritative, modern tech-editorial presence.
- **Positive Tracking on Micro Telemetry:** Monospace labels and badges feature open tracking (`+0.05em` to `+0.08em`) to guarantee crisp readability on micro viewports and dark surfaces.
- **Reading Comfort:** Inspector bodies utilize a strict 1.6 relative line-height to ensure complex ArXiv abstracts and code-heavy paragraphs remain effortless to parse during long sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a **4px baseline modular grid**, balancing high-density information architecture with editorial white-space discipline.

### Viewport Layout Modes
1. **Bento Intelligence Feed (Overview Mode):** Responsive auto-fill grid configured with `minmax(360px, 1fr)` columns and `1.125rem` (18px) gutters. Ideal for scanning hundreds of daily signals.
2. **Zero-Scroll Cockpit Flight Mode (Execution Mode):** Two-pane master-detail cockpit bounded strictly to `calc(100vh - 120px)`.
   - **Left Feed Stream:** `1.15fr` proportional width with internal virtual scroll.
   - **Right Inspector:** `0.85fr` sticky analytical station containing comprehensive provenance, model tokens, strategic impact analysis, and direct launch triggers.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Reflow
- **Desktop Wide (> 1200px):** Full master-detail cockpit or 3–4 column bento layout with `1.5rem` outer margin and 1560px max boundary.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 2-column bento; cockpit collapses to single-pane navigation with an overlay drawer inspector.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout, horizontal swipeable source spectrum rails, and sticky bottom micro telemetry bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed via calibrated multi-layer glass surfaces rather than simulated lighting shadows.

### The Double-Border Glass Technique
Each card and interactive surface relies on dual-stroke definition:
1. **Outer Boundary:** 1px solid hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
2. **Top Inner Highlight:** Inset `0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to simulate an overhead specular reflection on frosted glass.
3. **Diffused Ambient Occlusion:** Deep `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)` drop shadow to pull the card away from the background grid lattice.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Surface 0 (Canvas Void):** Deep Obsidian `#07080b` marked with a subtle 32px coordinate micro-grid.
- **Surface 1 (Base Container):** `rgba(15, 18, 27, 0.72)` with `16px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- **Surface 2 (Elevated Active / Hover):** `rgba(22, 27, 40, 0.85)` with increased border opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`).
- **Surface 3 (Flight Cockpit Focus):** `rgba(26, 32, 48, 0.95)` framed by an electric radar emerald atmospheric glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The design uses soft, structural corner geometry (Level 1) that communicates engineering precision while maintaining comfortable readability.

### Token Geometry Assignments
- **Core Elements (`rounded` / 4px - 6px):** Code snippets, status indicator badges, input fields, and keyboard command keycaps (`Ctrl K`).
- **Cards & Bento Units (`rounded-lg` / 8px - 10px):** Telemetry news cards, analytics charts, and modal inspector drawers.
- **Interactive Action Surfaces (`rounded-xl` / 12px - 16px):** Primary flight deck panels and high-level bento wrappers.
- **Pill Badges (`full` / 9999px):** Source spectrum filters, status beacon rings, and sentiment score capsules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Telemetry Harvest (`.btn-primary`):** 
  - Background: Gradient `linear-gradient(180deg, #182330 0%, #101924 100%)`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)` with an interior highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
  - Label: Monospace 12px / 600 weight in `#10b981`.
  - Hover: `transform: translateY(-1px)`, border-color `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.7)`, outer glow `0 0 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary Action (`.btn-secondary`):**
  - Translucent glass fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` with hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
  - Label: Monospace 12px in `#94a3b8`.
  - Hover: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, color `#f8fafc`.

### Signal & News Bento Cards
- **Structure:**
  - Header: Ingestion origin chip (e.g., GitHub, ArXiv, Hugging Face) paired with a priority score badge (`0.98 HIGH`).
  - Title: Outfit 17px semi-bold with `-0.01em` tracking, hover-linking to source.
  - Abstract: 3-line clamped summary in `#94a3b8`.
  - Insight Callout: Recessed container (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.05)`) with a 2px solid emerald left border displaying "Why It Matters" in mint luminescence (`#a7f3d0`).
  - Metadata Bar: Monospace tags for cluster topics, model badges, and external launch glyph.

### Category & Source Spectrum Filter Chips
- **Track & Pill:** Rendered along a recessed rail (`rgba(11, 14, 21, 0.85)`).
- **Inactive State:** Pill with translucent fill, muted icon, and count indicator.
- **Active State:** Solid elevated charcoal container (`#121622`) with an emerald label, top edge highlight, and highlighted count pill.

### Form Inputs & Search Command Bar
- **Search Command Well:** Recessed dark glass track with an embedded SVG search glyph on the left and a physical keycap shortcut badge (`Ctrl K` or `⌘ K`) on the right.
- **Focus Transition:** Border lightens to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.50)` with a 2px diffuse emerald glow ring. Text transitions to `#f8fafc`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Toggle Switches:** Recessed track with a 14px mechanical slider thumb that activates with a high-contrast emerald halo.
- **Status Indicators (Live Radar Beacon):** 8px circular indicator with an automated 2-second pulsating radar ripple ring (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)` to transparent) signaling live stream synchronicity.
